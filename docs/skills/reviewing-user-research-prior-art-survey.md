# `reviewing-user-research-prior-art-survey`

Judge a produced published-user-research prior-art artifact — a research vocabulary map or a
per-angle search output — against a twenty-seven-condition bar single-sourced with
`user-research-prior-art-survey`. An acceptance gate, not authoring.

## Why the bar lives here

`references/conditions.md` is the pair's single anchor. The producing skill points at it by name
and restates nothing normative. That arrangement exists because a bar duplicated across both
halves drifts: one document says `kept` counts results, the other says it counts rows, both look
right in isolation, and the disagreement only surfaces when an artifact is graded by the half
that did not write it.

## What it judges

**Mechanical conditions** are marked *(gated)* and discharged by one run of the co-installed
producer's validator. The reviewer does not restate them.

**Judgment conditions** are the point:

- Does the `method` axis name study designs, or topic terms wearing a method's clothes? A method
  group that restates the topic retrieves nothing new, and the map has lost the axis that
  reaches this literature at all.
- **Does the admitted source's full text really exist, and does it really state a method?** The
  two conjuncts fail in opposite directions — retrievable argument that reports no study, and a
  method section behind a wall — and a reviewer who checks one passes half the artifact.
- Does a crawl-delayed cell's selection record what it chose **not** to fetch?
- Is `provenance: extracted` honest, given the map is built before the search?
- Is a zero a zero, a failure, or a throttle?

## C13 is a coherence check, and says so

The central condition asks whether a zero is doing a failure's work — and the first version asked
the reviewer to judge whether the recorded status was *true*. A blind reviewer applying that
version approved a planted laundered-failure fixture, correctly: the evidence set is closed,
re-probing is disallowed, and a thoroughly laundered zero leaves no residue inside it.

So C13 now enumerates the five internal contradictions that discharge it — a sibling cell that
reached the same index minutes earlier, a retrieval summary that disagrees with the cell, a
registry posture the artifact contradicts, an incoherent cell record, a map that says otherwise —
and admits exactly one outside channel under an asymmetry: **a re-probe may be cited when it
FAILS, never when it succeeds.** A failed re-probe corroborates the producer; a successful one
proves only that conditions changed, and one of this survey's sources throttles on load nobody
can see.

The rewritten condition catches the fixture the old one approved. The genuinely unreachable case
— a launder that also reconciles the summary — is recorded as a **stated limit** rather than
pretended away, because a condition that promises more than its evidence set can deliver is worse
than one that is honest about its edge.

## Proportionality is a condition, not a disposition

**C27 — a thin-but-honest artifact is correct output for a thinly-researched domain.** Most
product scopes have very little research addressing them directly, several angles legitimately
return zeros, and revising for thinness invites padding. The question is never "is there enough
here?" but "is what is missing accounted for?" — revise only on a specific *unrecorded* gap.

## Evidence discipline

Every condition names what counts as grounds: the artifact, its schemas, the source registry, and
for a search output the map it came from. The reviewer's own knowledge of the literature is
explicitly not evidence — if a landmark paper seems missing, the finding is that the coverage
does not account for it, not that the reviewer knows its title. Findings are reported in one pass.

## Output

Exactly one verdict line — `VERDICT: approve` or `VERDICT: revise` — followed by findings, each
naming its condition number and quoting the artifact text that fails it.

Proven against three planted fixtures that pass the deterministic gate and are each wrong. A
blind reviewer with no knowledge that defects existed **revised all three** under the intended
conditions (C13, C18+C19, C17), and the same run surfaced a real defect in the producer's own
baseline fixture — shortlist counts in a `selection` that exceeded the rows the cell carried, so
retrieved items vanished from the record. That finding is now C20's accounting half, and the
fixture is fixed.
