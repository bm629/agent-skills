# What this reviewer reads, and what each source settles

Nine sources. Items **6 and 7** are the ones two conditions cannot be discharged without — the absent-input policy for C9 and the category vocabulary for C13 — and they are the two a reviewer most often skips, because the artifact looks judgeable without them. Named rather than counted from the end: an earlier revision said "the two before the last two", which pointed at items 5 and 6, and the ordering here is not the ordering in `SKILL.md`'s table.

1. **The artifact** — the map or the search output under review.
2. **The vocabulary map it was produced against** — without it the owed grid cannot be checked at
   all, and neither can an angle's `not_run`.
3. **The scope and classification the producer was handed** — `meta.classification` is a
   TRANSCRIPTION. Judged only against itself, a fabricated value reads exactly like a real one.
4. **`integrations-prior-art-survey/schemas/*.json`** — every field a reviewer judges carries a `description` saying what it is
   FOR. Read it before deciding a value is wrong.
5. **`integrations-prior-art-survey/references/source-registry.yaml`** — the caps, orderings, authority bands, `complete_listing`
   values and the excluded block. A `bound` is judged against this file, not against your sense of
   a reasonable number.
6. **`integrations-prior-art-survey/references/absent-input-policy.md`** — the nine catalog auth
   modes and which of them map to `null`. C9 cannot be discharged without it.
7. **`integrations-prior-art-survey/references/category-vocabulary.md`** — the seeded `category`
   vocabulary. C13 says "judge against that", and this is that.
8. **`integrations-prior-art-survey/references/angles/<angle>.md`** — the angle's own mechanism, sources, precondition and what
   it uniquely covers.
9. **`integrations-prior-art-survey/references/integration-vocabulary-map-guide.md`** — the probe's
   THREE checks. C20 judges the probe note against them, and `probe-record` only checks the note is
   non-empty.

The producer package is SELF-SUFFICIENT and does not read this one. Nothing here relaxes a duty
stated there.
