# Angle a3 — control-catalog and technical-standard retrieval

- **Conditional:** none — always-on angle.
- **Searches these axes:** `control-catalog`, `obligation-dimension`.
- **Sources:** `nist-oscal-content`, `nist-csrc`, `w3c`, `pci-ssc-library`, `fedramp-github`,
  `hl7-fhir`. **Fallback:** `nist-oscal-content`.
- **Cap:** 12 — **ordering:** catalog authority, then control family.

## Mechanism

Retrieve the machine-readable control catalogs and numbered standards that law incorporates by
reference, that a regulator names as an accepted means of compliance, or that a contractual scheme
imposes.

**Structured lookup, not text search.** Load the OSCAL catalog or profile JSON and select by
control family; for a baseline, take the instrument's OWN profile as its scoping mechanism. For
W3C, traverse the success-criterion list.

## Control ids are three grammars, not one

- **OSCAL lowercase-dotted** (`ac-1`, `at-2.2`) for NIST control ids. `AT-2(2)` is the SAME control
  under a different spelling, and mixing the two silently splits a merge group in two. The
  lowercase-dotted form wins because it is what a validator can check against the catalog JSON.
- **WCAG success-criterion numbers** (`1.4.3`) are their own grammar.
- **PCI requirement numbers** (`12.10.1`) are a third.

Record which vocabulary a control id belongs to. A blanket rule over one of them refuses the other
two.

## Why this cap

The corpus is a small governed set of named catalogs, not an index; a larger cap invites padding
with catalogs nothing in scope references.

**The cap counts CATALOGS.** The SP 800-53 LOW baseline inside ONE of them resolves to 149 control
ids — those are records, not rows, and sizing the cap against 149 would size it against the wrong
unit.

## PCI text is not retrievable, and that is a finding

The library index answers 200 and its robots is permissive. The DOCUMENTS live on
`docs-prv.pcisecuritystandards.org`, which returns **403 on the PDF and 403 on its own
robots.txt** — a blocked host, not a licence gate. A PCI record carries `text_retrievable: blocked`,
cites requirement numbers, and quotes nothing. Never paraphrase a clause you could not read.

## What only this angle covers

The requirement already in **implementable** form, carrying an externally-owned stable control id.
That vocabulary is what a later merge joins on; without it the merge would have to match on prose,
which does not work.
