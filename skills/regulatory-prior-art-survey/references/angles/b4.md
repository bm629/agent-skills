# Angle b4 — cross-border-transfer and residency-instrument retrieval

- **Conditional:** `scale.geo_distribution in {multi-region, global, edge}`.
  **Anchor:** `scale.geo_distribution`.
- **Searches these axes:** `transfer-mechanism`, `jurisdiction`.
- **Sources:** `eu-cellar`, `edpb`, `ico`. **Fallback:** `edpb`.
- **Cap:** 18 — **ordering:** adequacy status, then jurisdiction reach.

## Not tautological

`regulatory.applies` says nothing about deployment topology. A regulated single-region product
falsifies this predicate.

## The anchor is a PROXY, and it is declared as one

The field that names where data must live — `infrastructure.data_residency` — is declared with
**zero properties**, so any predicate over it is a presence test on an unconstrained object. The
field that names the residency requirement cannot gate the angle about residency requirements.

So this angle anchors on deployment topology instead. **What that misses:** a single-region product
serving cross-border USERS. Its own deployment does not cross a border, so this predicate is false,
and the transfer question is still real for it. That residual is covered by `a1`'s sector
sub-topic, and it is recorded here rather than left for someone to discover.

## Mechanism

Retrieve the **transfer-instrument corpus** — adequacy decisions, standard-contractual-clause
implementing decisions, national transfer mechanisms, and the assessment guidance around them.
These are a separate register of documents with their own identifiers, **not a chapter of any one
privacy law**, and looking for them inside a privacy regulation finds the empowering article and
none of the instruments.

Enumerate the adequacy register for the destination jurisdictions the scope names, resolve each to
its own CELEX, then retrieve the supervisory guidance on the assessment a controller must run where
no adequacy decision covers the destination.

## Why this cap

The adequacy register plus the SCC implementing decisions is an enumerable corpus in the low tens.
Sized against it, with the ordering above deciding what a truncation would drop.

## What only this angle covers

The constraint that decides **where the database can physically live** — an architecture decision
made in week one and expensive in month twelve.
