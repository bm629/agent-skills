# Angle b5 — financial and payments-instrument retrieval

- **Conditional:** `business.platform.type = "payments-network"` OR
  (`archetype.primary = "api-service"` AND `regulatory.frameworks` names a financial instrument).
  **Anchors:** `business.platform.type`, `archetype.primary`.
- **Searches these axes:** `instrument`, `control-catalog`.
- **Sources:** `eu-cellar`, `eba`, `pci-ssc-library`, `ecfr-api`. **Fallback:** `ecfr-api`.
- **Cap:** 18 — **ordering:** instrument bindingness, then recency.

## Not tautological

Neither anchor is mentioned by `regulatory.applies`.

## The frameworks leg is a WIDENER, and its limit is stated

`regulatory.frameworks` is an unnormalized free-string array, and membership is EXACT — so
`PCI DSS` does not match `PCI-DSS`. That miss costs a widening, never a firing, because every
required-rooted anchor sits on the other disjunct. Recorded rather than papered over.

## Mechanism

Retrieve the payment-services and financial-operational-resilience instruments **plus the
contractual card-scheme standard**, which is not law and binds anyway.

**Resolve the instrument, then FOLLOW ITS DELEGATED ACTS.** PSD2's operative security requirements
live in EBA regulatory technical standards, not in the directive — an extraction that stops at the
directive misses every concrete control. This is the angle where stopping at the named instrument
produces a confident, empty result.

## PCI text is not retrievable

The library index answers 200; the documents live on a separate host that returns 403 on the PDF
and on its own robots.txt. A PCI record carries `text_retrievable: blocked`, cites requirement
numbers, and quotes nothing.

## Why this cap

PSD2, DORA and the card-scheme standard, plus the delegated acts each pulls in. The delegated acts
are what make this larger than the named-instrument count suggests.

## An honest limit, recorded

This anchor fires for a product that IS a payments network, not for one that merely accepts card
payments. That much larger population is invisible to the capability map, and is covered only by
`a1`'s sector sub-topic.

## What only this angle covers

Strong-customer-authentication mechanics, ICT third-party-risk register duties, incident-reporting
windows measured in hours, and the only contractually-binding technical standard in the survey.
