# Angle a2 — regulator guidance and enforcement-decision retrieval

- **Conditional:** none — always-on angle.
- **Searches these axes:** `instrument`, `obligation-dimension`, `jurisdiction`.
- **Sources:** `edpb`, `ico`, `ftc`, `hhs-ocr-breach-portal`, `cppa`, `ca-oag`,
  `federal-register-api`, `enforcementtracker`. **Fallback:** `federal-register-api`.
- **Cap:** 20 — **ordering:** regulator seniority, then decision recency.

## Mechanism

Retrieve the supervisory authority's OWN guidance, opinions and decision registers — the layer
where an abstract statutory duty ("appropriate technical and organisational measures") becomes a
concrete technical expectation, and where a penalty figure becomes attributable to a decision
rather than to a statutory maximum.

Enter by the body's own guidance index or decision register, **never by site search**. For an
enforcement decision, record the decision reference and the obligation it turned on — **never the
headline fine.**

## Two traps on these hosts, both measured

**`edpb` restructured, and its old URL is a silent redirect.** Every legacy `/our-work-tools/…`
guidance path 301s to `/public-consultations_en` — a DIFFERENT page that answers **200**. A run
walking the old URL records consultations as guidance and never sees an error. Use
`/documents_en`; the Art. 60 register is at `/registers/register-of-final-one-stop-shop-decisions_en`.

**`ftc` and `enforcementtracker` answer on the USER AGENT.** Both return 403 to a default agent and
200 to a browser one, at the same byte counts. That is a property of the request, not of the row —
record the agent you used in that cell's `queries` entry, alongside the URL, because a status
without its request is not evidence. The registry's `probe_method` for those two rows gives the
agent string; a query line that omits it cannot be re-issued.

## Authority, and what a tracker is for

`enforcementtracker` is **authority tier 4** and a navigational aid only: use it to FIND a decision,
then cite the decision at `edpb` or `ico`. It is never the source of a penalty claim.

## Why this cap

Sized on the corpus of supervisory bodies this survey can reach — a small set of hosts, each
publishing a bounded guidance index. `ico` declares `Crawl-delay: 6` and `ftc` 5, which bounds the
RATE and not the count; do not confuse the two.

## What only this angle covers

The operative interpretation, and the only channel that grounds an enforcement claim in a published
decision. Also the only source of a regulator's stated **accepted means** of compliance, which is
what makes an obligation implementable.
