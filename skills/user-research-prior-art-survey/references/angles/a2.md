# a2 — practitioner-research corpus retrieval

**Trigger:** always. **Cap:** 12. **Sources:** `nngroup`, `baymard-articles`,
`govuk-service-manual`.
**Fallback:** `govuk-service-manual`.

## Mechanism

Retrieve applied research already translated into design recommendations, by the map's
`user-population`, `task` and `component` vocabulary.

**Select from an index FIRST.** The primary source declares a 60-second crawl delay, so
breadth-first fetching spends the entire run on retrieval and reads nothing. Enumerate the
sitemap or a section index (which costs one fetch), shortlist on the titles, then fetch only the
shortlist — typically three to eight documents. Every candidate identified and deliberately not
fetched is recorded in the cell's `selection`, which is what makes the shortlist judgeable
rather than arbitrary.

## Query strategy

Enter by sitemap or by a known article URL. **Never enter by the source's own site search** —
the primary source disallows `/search/` outright, and a query against a disallowed path is a
terms breach whether or not it returns results.

The practitioner corpus with a subscription is **two registry entries**, and only one of them is
a source of this angle. `baymard-articles` is the free article corpus and is reachable;
`baymard-premium` is excluded on its terms, and the validator rejects a cell or a fallback naming
it. Reach the article corpus; do not treat the gated half as a coverage gap to work around.

## Unique coverage

Findings stated as actionable guidance in the form a product document can consume. Academic work
states effects; this angle states what to do about them. A survey with only a1 produces a
register a designer cannot act on, and a survey with only a2 produces one nobody can check.

## Failure modes

- **Ignoring the declared delay.** The delay is a source's stated condition of use, and honouring
  it is not optional. A cell that fetched faster than the declared spacing is a terms breach the
  coverage record will not show unless the `selection` says what it did.
- **Admitting an essay.** Practitioner writing is a mix of reported studies and argument. Full
  text is retrievable for both, so the admission turns entirely on the second conjunct: does the
  document state a method? An article that reports no study goes to `unadmitted` with that
  reason, however persuasive it is.
- **Treating a recommendation as a finding.** "Use larger touch targets" is guidance. The finding
  is the study behind it, and if the article does not name one, this angle has not found a
  finding.
- **Entering by site search.** See above — disallowed on the primary source.

## Fallback

`govuk-service-manual`: openly licensed, published without a crawl delay, and not a commercial
asset, so it can neither gate nor rate-limit the angle away. Narrower in domain coverage, but it
cannot fail for the reasons the others can.

## Cap

12, bounded by the 60-second delay rather than by the corpus: twelve fetches is roughly ten
minutes of retrieval before anything is read. A larger cap would be a number this angle cannot
reach in a run, which is a promise rather than a limit.
