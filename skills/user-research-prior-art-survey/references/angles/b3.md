# b3 — platform usage-research retrieval

**Trigger:** conditional — `archetype.primary = "mobile-app"` OR `archetype.secondary` contains
it. **Anchor:** `archetype.primary` (required). **Widening leg:** `archetype.secondary`
(optional). **Cap:** 20. **Sources:** `android-developers`, `apple-developer`.
**Fallback:** `crossref`.

## Mechanism

Retrieve the platform vendors' own published usage research and the research-backed sections of
their design guidance, for the scope's `task` and `component` vocabulary.

Both vendors publish two very different kinds of page under one roof: guidance that asserts, and
guidance that reports. Only the second is admissible here. A page that says "use a bottom sheet
for secondary actions" states a house style; a page that reports what proportion of users reach
a control at a given screen position states a finding.

## The optional leg

`archetype.secondary` is optional and sits BESIDE the required `archetype.primary`, so the
predicate always evaluates and the optional leg can only ADD firings. It is recorded as a
widening leg, never as an anchor. A scope whose primary archetype is something else but which
declares mobile as secondary correctly fires this angle; a scope that declares no secondary
correctly does not.

## Query strategy

Enter by a known guidance URL, not by site search — one vendor disallows `/search/` and
`/reference/` outright. Cross each `component` term with the platform's own name for it, which
frequently differs from the map's canonical term; record both forms in the query, verbatim.

## Unique coverage

Platform-conditioned behaviour — how people use an application **on this platform**, as opposed
to how they use the pattern in the abstract. A reachability finding is a property of a device
held in a hand, not of a control.

## Failure modes

- **Admitting a house style as a finding.** The single commonest error here. Vendor guidance is
  written in the imperative whether or not a study stands behind it.
- **Letting one vendor stand for the platform.** Two vendors publish independently and disagree;
  a register carrying only one has a coverage gap its cells will not show unless the other's
  cells are recorded as searched.
- **Missing the version.** Platform guidance is revised silently. A finding read from vendor
  documentation is a point-in-time claim and the cell's `queries` are the only record of when.
- **Firing on a non-mobile scope.** The trigger rests on a required field; a scope with neither
  leg holding yields `holds: false` and this angle does not run.

## Fallback

`crossref`. Vendor documentation is not archived anywhere this survey controls, so when a vendor
site changes shape the general index is the only channel that reaches comparable work — mobile
interaction research carries DOIs.

## Cap

20. Two vendors publishing a bounded set of research-backed guidance pages; 20 is the size of
that set for one scope's components, and a larger cap would pull in guidance carrying no study
at all.
