# b2 — assistive-technology research retrieval

**Trigger:** always applicable — this angle is not gated.
**Why not conditional:** its previous anchor was `ui.has_ui`, which this type's trigger leg 1
entails, so it fired for every leg-1 project while claiming to be gated. The honest predicate
rests on `ui.accessibility.required_level`, optional inside an optional object, which fails
closed instead.
**Level selection:** a declared level sharpens which conformance level is targeted; absent one,
AA is the default. **Cap:** 18.
**Source:** `w3c-wai`. **Fallback:** `crossref`.

## Mechanism

Retrieve published research on how assistive-technology users operate the scope's named
components. **Query by `component` and `task` together** — assistive-technology research is
indexed by widget ("screen reader" plus "combobox"), not by task alone, and a task-only query
returns general accessibility advocacy rather than interaction evidence.

## The trigger, and why it has two legs

`ui.accessibility` is not a required block, and `required_level` is not required within it. A
predicate resting only on the declared level would therefore fail closed — silently, and for
exactly the products that need this angle most, since a discovery pass that simply omitted the
accessibility block looks identical to one that considered it and found nothing.

So the required-rooted leg is `ui.has_ui`, and an undeclared level **defaults to AA**, which is
what the downstream design consumer requires unconditionally. A declared level overrides the
default. The optional leg is recorded as a widening leg, never as the anchor.

## Query strategy

Cross each `component` term with a task and with the assistive technology by name. Draft
material is out of scope and the source excludes its own drafts by robots, which is correct for
a survey that must cite published positions — do not route around it.

## Unique coverage

The only angle whose user population is defined by how people interact rather than by who they
are, and the only one that reaches the evidence behind an accessibility requirement rather than
the requirement itself.

## Failure modes

- **Citing the requirement instead of the research.** A success criterion states what must hold.
  This angle is looking for the study that shows what happens when it does not. Both are useful;
  only the second is a finding.
- **Assuming the angle does not apply because no level was declared.** That is the exact failure
  the second trigger leg exists to prevent.
- **Querying by task alone.** Returns advocacy and policy, not interaction evidence.
- **Treating one assistive technology as all of them.** Screen-reader evidence does not transfer
  to switch access or magnification, and a record that does not name the technology cannot be
  weighed later.

## Fallback

`crossref`. The standards body publishes its research pages as a small governed set; when they
are unreachable, the same work is reachable by DOI through the general index.

## Cap

18. The corpus is a small governed set of published research and requirement-rationale pages,
not an open index — 18 covers it without inviting padding from unrelated results.
