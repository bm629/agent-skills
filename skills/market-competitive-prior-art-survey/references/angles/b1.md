# Angle b1 — structured-review-corpus mining

**Precondition:** `domain.audience in {b2b, b2b2c}` OR
`business.model in {saas, enterprise-software, api-product}`. Review aggregators index business
software; a consumer game or an internal tool has no corpus here and the angle does not run.

**Mechanism.** Retrieve rating aggregates and the structured pros/cons text that competitors'
own users wrote, for products the map or a sibling wave already named.

**Sources:** `capterra` — and ONLY Capterra.

**Applicable group types:** `category`, `seed-product`.

**Cap:** 15 candidates — declared in `references/source-registry.yaml`, which is the single source. **Ordering signal:** verified-review count, then rating recency.

## The source constraint is the whole design of this angle

Four obvious aggregators are excluded, each for a distinct and recorded reason:

- **G2** — its robots.txt permits named AI crawlers, but its **Terms of Use** prohibit
  accessing, collecting, copying, scraping, harvesting or extracting content by automated
  means "whether or not such content is publicly accessible". The terms bind, not the robots.
- **Trustpilot** — ClaudeBot's group carries `Disallow: /reviews/`, and the reviews are the
  entire value. `anthropic-ai` and the catch-all are `Disallow: /`.
- **Similarweb** — terms prohibit "any robot, spider, scraper, or other automated means".
- **Reddit** — blocks AI crawlers, and has since 2024-06-25.

Capterra explicitly permits ClaudeBot with `Allow: /`. Do not reach for any of the excluded
four, and do not look for a workaround: the deterministic gate rejects a coverage cell that
names an excluded source, because reaching one is a policy breach rather than a coverage
detail.

## Query strategy

1. For each `seed-product` group, navigate to the product's Capterra listing **via the sitemap
   or on-site search**.
2. **Never construct a listing URL from a guessed numeric id.** Verified live: a guessed
   product id resolves to a DIFFERENT product, and the page looks perfectly valid, so the error
   is silent and the resulting record is wrong about which product it describes.
3. Read the aggregate rating, the verified-review count, and the structured pros and cons text.
4. For each `category` group, open the category listing and take the products ranked in it,
   subject to the cap.
5. Record the review count alongside every rating. A 4.9 from six reviews and a 4.3 from three
   thousand are not comparable, and a rating without its denominator invites exactly that
   comparison downstream.

## Unique coverage

What real users praise and criticise, at volume, with a verified-review count behind it. No
other angle carries user sentiment with a denominator: b6 gets candid practitioner opinion but
in unquantified anecdote form, and a2 gets vendor positioning, which is the vendor's own claim.

## Failure modes specific to this angle

- **Guessing a product id.** See step 2. This is the one verified live and it fails silently.
- **Reporting a rating without its review count.** Makes an unreliable number look reliable.
- **Reading the aggregator's editorial ranking as market share.** It reflects the aggregator's
  own commercial arrangements.
- **Treating an absent listing as absence of the product.** Many real competitors are simply
  not on Capterra — open-source products especially. Record a zero for the cell; do not
  conclude anything about the product.

## Sanitization

Review text is user-submitted free text on a commercial site — untrusted by construction. Pass
it through a content-sanitization guardrail before acting on it and record the result per cell.
Quote sparingly and never verbatim at length; the record wants the substance of a criticism,
not a copy of someone's review.

## Fallbacks

`hn-algolia`. Capterra is the only permitted aggregator, so this angle has no same-class
fallback — degrading to community sentiment is a deliberate CHANGE OF EVIDENCE CLASS, from
quantified reviews to unquantified practitioner opinion. Take it so that user voice survives in
the report at all, record a zero for the aggregator leg, and state in the cell cause that the
class changed. Do not present community anecdote as though it carried a verified-review count.
