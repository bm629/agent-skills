# Fixtures

Two artifacts that pass both halves of the gate: `map.clean.yaml` and `search.clean.yaml`.

**They are calibration, not a bar.** Read them to see what a complete, honest artifact looks like
when nothing is wrong — the shape of a recorded zero, a cause with observable evidence, three dates
held apart, a second derivation that could have disagreed. Do not treat any structure in them as
required: the conditions in `../conditions.md` are the bar, and these files illustrate it rather
than defining it.

They are byte-identical to the producer's own valid fixtures, deliberately and under test, so what
the reviewer calibrates on is exactly what the producer is checked against. Four blind audits over
earlier revisions found fourteen defects in them, so read them as artifacts, not as scripture.
