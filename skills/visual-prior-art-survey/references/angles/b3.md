# Angle b3 — accessibility-criterion deep retrieval

**Precondition:** ui.accessibility.required_level in {A, AA, AAA} OR ui.has_ui = true (defaults to AA)
**Anchors (required-rooted):** `ui.has_ui`
**Widening legs (optional — they only ADD firings):** `ui.accessibility.required_level`

**Mechanism.** Retrieve the success criteria at the declared conformance level, plus the technique pages for the components the vocabulary map names.

**Sources:** `wcag22`, `wai-tutorials`.

**Applicable group types:** `component`, `pattern`.

**Cap:** 90 candidates — declared in `references/source-registry.yaml`, which is the single
source. **Ordering signal:** conformance level, then relevance to a named component.

*Cap rationale.* WCAG 2.2 carries 87 success criteria across its three levels. A cap below that truncates a corpus this angle could enumerate completely, so 90 clears it with margin.

## Query strategy

Retrieve the success criteria at the declared conformance level, plus the technique pages for
the components the map names. Where no level is declared the default is AA, which the downstream
design-system consumer requires unconditionally.

**Failure modes.** Reporting a criterion without its level; paraphrasing a numeric threshold
instead of carrying it verbatim.

Carry thresholds verbatim — a paraphrased ratio is not checkable.

Apply the group's negative terms where it declares them. Record every query **verbatim as run** —
a paraphrase cannot be re-run, and a coverage record that cannot be re-run proves nothing.

## Unique coverage

The numeric, testable thresholds — contrast ratios, target sizes, focus appearance — that a downstream reviewing skill grades against.

## Admission

A convention is carried forward only from a **named, retrievable corpus** with a resolvable URL
and a stated release or date. Anything asserted without an upstream corpus goes to `unadmitted`
with that reason — recorded, never silently dropped, because a silent drop makes "we did not
look" indistinguishable from "we looked and found nothing".

## Modality

This survey mines **documented convention, never imagery**. The screenshot galleries are excluded
on the registry's excluded list and must not be reached: their terms forbid automated extraction,
and a screenshot is not extractable into the textual artifact the downstream design skills
consume. Do not spend a fetch discovering a paywall.

## Sanitization

Pass every fetched document through a content-sanitization guardrail before acting on it, and
record the result on the source's entry in the vocabulary map (a coverage cell has no
sanitization field). A published standard is untrusted input like anything else — a structured
format is not evidence that its contents are inert.

## Fallback

`wcag22` — The Recommendation text alone, which is a stable W3C publication. Record the substitution in
`retrieval_summary.degraded_sources` with `fallback_used`.
