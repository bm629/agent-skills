# b3 — complementor-side experience mining

**Trigger:** conditional — `business.platform.type in {marketplace, app-store, dev-platform}`.
**Anchor (required-rooted):** `business.platform.type`. **Cap:** 10 — **ordering:** whether the
platform hosts the forum itself, then thread recency. **Sources:** `chromium-ext-group`,
`vscode-docs-repo`. **Fallback:** `chromium-ext-group`.

## Why this predicate is not a tautology

Three of the seven live values — the ones with a complementor population large enough to leave a
public record. **Falsifying witness:** `business.platform.type = media` satisfies the type trigger
and leaves this angle false.

## Mechanism

Mine the complementor's own account of building on the platform: review turnarounds, breaking
changes, migration cost, what the documentation omitted. **From forums the PLATFORM hosts** — its
own mailing list, issue tracker or discussion board — never from aggregators or news coverage.

## The evidential caution this angle carries

This is the only angle whose corpus is unstructured, self-selected and adversarially incomplete:
people post when something breaks. A complaint is evidence that a complaint was made, not evidence
of a rate. Record it as the former.

Two guards follow. Quote verbatim with a locator, because a paraphrase of a forum post is a
paraphrase of an anecdote. And never aggregate — "several developers reported" is a count with no
denominator and no counting frame, which is the shape of an unfalsifiable claim.

## Unique coverage

The gap between what a platform documents and what building on it is actually like. No first-party
source reports that, and no other angle looks for it.
