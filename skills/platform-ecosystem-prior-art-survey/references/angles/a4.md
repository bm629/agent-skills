# a4 — platform-change forensics

**Trigger:** always applicable. **Cap:** 12 — **ordering:** enforcement date, then
announcement-to-enforcement interval. **Sources:** `chromium-ext-group`, `chrome-mv2-timeline`,
`mozilla-policies`. **Fallback:** `chromium-ext-group`.

## Mechanism

Read migration announcements, deprecation timelines and their archives. **The interval between
announcement and enforcement is the most useful number a platform's history yields**, and no
sibling survey type has an equivalent angle.

Record `announced_on` and `enforced_on` as separate fields. A prose sentence saying "they gave
about four years" cannot be divided by anything.

## The trap: a page that describes a past event in the future tense

One timeline in this corpus lists a milestone that has already passed while still presenting it
prospectively, and its own claimed modification date is weeks stale. **Write the outcome in the
past tense and record that the page has not been updated.** The mismatch between what a page says
and when it says it is itself evidence about how the platform maintains its documentation.

## Unique coverage

The only angle that reads a platform's history rather than its present state. A platform's
willingness to break complementors is not visible in its current docs at all.
