# Writing the synthesis report

`decision-index.yaml` is the machine half and the build-phase handoff; `report.md` is what a human
reads beside it. Every figure in the report carries the extract record it came from.

## The nine sections are FIXED, in this order

1. **Extension model and attachment surface** — L1, L2, L3, L7.
2. **Permission and trust model** — L1, L4.
3. **Developer experience and onboarding funnel** — L6.
4. **Commercial terms and monetization** — L5, L2. Every figure dated; anything past the staleness
   window marked as such.
5. **Discovery, ranking and categorisation** — L1, L2, L9. **Expect an honest zero here.**
6. **Review, certification and enforcement** — L4, plus the enforcement clauses.
7. **Regulatory and gatekeeper exposure** — the regulatory records read through the
   obligation-to-mechanism mapping.
8. **Growth path: build first, defer, and when to migrate** — L3, L7, L8. Every deferral names its
   trigger.
9. **Coverage and absence receipt** *(mandatory)* — L9.

A section whose angle the trigger EXCLUDED renders `not-applicable: <the predicate>`. Not an empty
heading, and not silence: silence reads as "we looked and found nothing", and nobody looked.

## What does not go in the report

**No cross-platform average of a contractual term.** Platform types differ, and an average across
them is the mechanism by which a number nobody measured becomes an industry standard.

**No divergence resolved by dropping a source.** If the disagreement partitions on platform type,
say so and recommend accordingly. If it does not, present the tradeoff and refuse to pick — the
survey's job is to make the choice legible, not to make it.

**No sandbox recommendation without its review cost.** The two are joined for a reason.

**No architecture.** Which of these decisions to take is the downstream document's job, working
from the index.

## Grounding

Every claim names the record it rests on, and every contractual figure carries the date it was true.
An undated commercial term in this domain is a claim about a moment nobody can locate — and the two
platform changes this survey measured moved inside months.
