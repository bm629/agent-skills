# Angle a2 — dataset and training-corpus enumeration

- **Conditional:** none — always-on angle.
- **Searches these axes:** `ml-task`, `modality`, `domain-term`, `dataset`.
- **Sources:** `huggingface-hub-api`, `huggingface-hub-pages`, `openml-api`, `zenodo-records`. **Fallback:** `openml-api`.
- **Cap:** 30 — **ordering:** whether the dataset is cited by a benchmark, then downloads.

## Mechanism

Query dataset registries by task, modality and domain terms. The Croissant document was this angle's richest channel and is GATED as of 2026-09-01 — provenance, annotation and PII fields that were READ are no longer readable key-less. Record what the listing and card carry, and record the absence of the rest as a finding rather than inventing it.

## Why this cap

Datasets per task are far fewer than models; 30 clears the real set for most tasks.

## Why this fallback

Key-less JSON with numeric ids, and the only dataset channel here with a stable API contract.

## What only this angle covers

The only angle that surveys what you would TRAIN or EVALUATE ON. a1 finds models; a3 finds
results; this finds the corpora underneath both, and a scope with no usable dataset has a different
answer to the build-vs-buy question than one with several.

**This angle lost its richest channel.** The Croissant document HuggingFace auto-generates for
every Hub dataset carried provenance, annotation process, bias notes and PII flags as READ fields —
and it returns 401 to a key-less client as of 2026-09-01. Those fields are not gone from the world,
they are gone from this survey. Record their ABSENCE as a finding on the candidate; never infer a
provenance claim the card does not make.

**A dataset's licence decides adoption more often than its size.** Record it verbatim as the source
states it, including "unstated" — a normalised guess is a legal claim we are not entitled to make.
