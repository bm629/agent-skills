# Angle b1 — training and fine-tuning cost retrieval (conditional)

- **Trigger:** conditional — `data_ml.ml_involvement in {fine-tunes, trains-from-scratch, multi-model} — the three values whose projects actually pay for a training run`
- **Anchor (required-rooted):** `data_ml.ml_involvement`.
- **Searches these axes:** `ml-task`, `modality`, `method`.
- **Sources:** `mlcommons`, `artificial-analysis`, `huggingface-hub-api`, `ngc-catalog`. **Fallback:** `mlcommons`.
- **Cap:** 20 — **ordering:** whether the figure is measured or estimated, then recency.

## Mechanism

Price a training or fine-tuning run from published figures. Model SCALE is the search axis here and rides as expansions of a method group. An estimate is not a measurement, and the difference is recorded rather than averaged away.

## Why this cap

Published training-cost figures are rare; 20 exceeds what the corpus carries.

## Why this fallback

The only source publishing training results under stated, auditable submission rules.

## Why this predicate is not a tautology

No widener. `uses-pre-trained` satisfies the TYPE trigger and leaves this angle false, which is the common case and is exactly the discrimination the gate exists to make. A widener that admitted it would make the angle always-on and the gate decorative.

The type-level trigger is `data_ml.ml_involvement != 'none'`. This angle names a
PROPER SUBSET of what that admits, so a project can satisfy the type and leave this
angle false — which is the discrimination the gate exists to make. An angle whose
predicate merely restated the type trigger would fire on every survey and the
conditional marking would be decorative.

## What only this angle covers

The only angle that prices the BUILD side, and the one that makes the ladder a ladder: without a
training cost, "fine-tune" and "use an API" are compared on capability alone.

**Model SCALE is the search axis here**, and it rides as expansions of a `method` group — `7B`,
`70B`, `base`, `large` are variants of one architecture, not separate methods.

**An estimate is not a measurement.** A vendor's "typically under $X" and a submitted MLPerf result
are different kinds of claim, and averaging them produces a number with no frame. Record which you
have, with its authority, and let the synthesis wave weigh them.
