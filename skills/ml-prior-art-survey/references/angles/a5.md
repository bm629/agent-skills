# Angle a5 — hosted-inference catalogue and price retrieval

- **Conditional:** none — always-on angle.
- **Searches these axes:** `ml-task`.
- **Sources:** `openai-platform-docs`, `google-ai-docs`, `aws-docs`, `ms-learn`, `artificial-analysis`. **Fallback:** `artificial-analysis`.
- **Cap:** 20 — **ordering:** whether the price is vendor-published, then recency of the page.

## Mechanism

Walk the vendor model and pricing pages. The vendors are SOURCES, not vocabulary — this angle searches ml-task terms against them. A price is a dated claim about a document and moves without notice, which is why `as_of` matters more here than anywhere else.

## Why this cap

Four major vendor catalogues plus one independent aggregator; 20 clears them.

## Why this fallback

Independent cross-vendor measurement, so a vendor page that moves does not take the comparison with it.

## What only this angle covers

The only angle that prices the BUY side. Every other angle surveys something you would host;
this surveys what someone else will run for you, and the use-an-API rung of the ladder is
undecidable without it.

**The vendors are SOURCES, not vocabulary.** This angle searches `ml-task` terms against the vendor
catalogues. There is deliberately no `vendor` group type: the names already live in the registry as
source ids, and minting groups for them would put the same strings in two places and let them
drift.

**A price is the most perishable fact in this survey.** Two of these catalogues moved host or path
within eleven days of being verified. Record `as_of` from the page's own effective date where it
states one, `null` where it does not, and never from the fetch — a price stamped with the day you
read it is a fact about your calendar.
