# Angle a3 — leaderboard and evaluation-result retrieval

- **Conditional:** none — always-on angle.
- **Searches these axes:** `ml-task`, `benchmark`.
- **Sources:** `helm-github`, `mlcommons`, `arena-leaderboards`, `pwc-archive`. **Fallback:** `mlcommons`.
- **Cap:** 25 — **ordering:** independence of the evaluator, then recency of the result.

## Mechanism

Read published third-party evaluation tables. A rank is a CLAIM under a stated evaluation, on a stated split, at a stated date — record the benchmark and the split always, and the date where the table gives one. An undated table is a fact about the table — `measured_on: null` — never a reason to drop the result. The lead channel this type was designed around is dead, so treat every table as a document that may move.

## Why this cap

Published evaluation tables are few and largely fixed; 25 covers the live suites.

## Why this fallback

A standards body publishing dated, versioned result sets under stated submission rules.

## What only this angle covers

The only angle that carries a MEASURED comparison. Everything else records what a document
claims about itself; this records what a third party measured under a stated protocol.

**A rank is not a quality signal.** It is a claim under a stated evaluation, on a stated split, at
a stated date. The schema requires the benchmark and the split because a leaderboard position with
no split is not comparable to anything; the date is recorded where the table gives one and `null`
where it does not — `test` and `validation` are different numbers, and a table that does not
say which is a table you cannot use.

**Read the rendered leaderboard pages, never the APIs behind them.** The arena host allows the leaderboard paths and disallows `/api/`, `/nextjs-api/` and `/_next/`. You are the only angle that searches it, so this warning lives here or nowhere.

**The lead channel this type was designed around is dead.** Papers With Code 302s to an unrelated
feed and its leaderboard corpus is gone. Treat every remaining table as a document that may move:
record what you read and when, and prefer the archived snapshot to a live page you cannot
re-fetch.
