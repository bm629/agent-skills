# Angle b2 — safety and responsible-AI evaluation retrieval (conditional)

- **Trigger:** conditional — `regulatory.applies = true OR data_ml.eu_ai_act.risk_level in {unacceptable, high}`
- **Anchor (required-rooted):** `regulatory.applies`.
- **Searches these axes:** `ml-task`, `benchmark`, `harm-category`.
- **Sources:** `helm-github`, `mlcommons`, `huggingface-hub-pages`, `eurlex-ai-act`, `nist-ai-rmf`. **Fallback:** `mlcommons`.
- **Cap:** 20 — **ordering:** independence of the evaluator, then whether the harm axis is named explicitly.

## Mechanism

Retrieve the safety, bias and robustness evaluations published FOR the models in scope. The regulatory instruments are PROVENANCE, never records — a regulation cannot be adopted as an ML option, and filing one as a candidate is a category error this angle is most prone to.

## Why this cap

Safety evaluation suites are a small, named set.

## Why this fallback

Publishes safety-relevant result sets under the same auditable rules as its performance ones.

## Why this predicate is not a tautology

`data_ml.eu_ai_act.applies` was considered as a widener and REJECTED: the upstream coherence axiom asserts eu_ai_act.applies implies regulatory.applies, so it cannot fire while the anchor is false and adds no firing. `risk_level` is not implied by the axiom and does widen. Recorded so a later reader can tell considered-and-rejected from overlooked.

The type-level trigger is `data_ml.ml_involvement != 'none'`. This angle names a
PROPER SUBSET of what that admits, so a project can satisfy the type and leave this
angle false — which is the discrimination the gate exists to make. An angle whose
predicate merely restated the type trigger would fire on every survey and the
conditional marking would be decorative.

## What only this angle covers

The only angle that asks what the model does WRONG, and the only one whose absence is itself a
regulatory fact for some scopes.

**The instruments are PROVENANCE, never records.** A regulation cannot be adopted as an ML option.
Filing the AI Act as a candidate is the category error this angle is most prone to — cite it as the
reason a harm axis is in scope, and let the CANDIDATE be the model or the evaluation suite.

**An absent safety evaluation is a finding.** "The card publishes no bias evaluation on any
protected attribute" is evidence a decision-maker needs; an empty field is a hole they will read as
an oversight.
