# Angle b4 — on-device and constrained-runtime retrieval (conditional)

- **Trigger:** conditional — `scale.geo_distribution = "edge" OR archetype.primary in {mobile-app, embedded-iot, desktop-app, browser-extension}`
- **Anchor (required-rooted):** `scale.geo_distribution`, `archetype.primary`.
- **Searches these axes:** `modality`, `runtime-format`.
- **Sources:** `huggingface-hub-api`, `ngc-catalog`. **Fallback:** `ngc-catalog`.
- **Cap:** 20 — **ordering:** whether the artifact ships in a runtime format, then measured on-device footprint.

## Mechanism

Walk the Hub by ARTIFACT FORMAT and quantization rather than by task: an on-device candidate is defined by what it runs on, and a task-first walk returns the same server-class models the other angles already carry.

## Why this cap

Quantized and on-device artifact families are a bounded set per task.

## Why this fallback

Vendor-optimized artifacts are where constrained-runtime builds are actually published.

## Why this predicate is not a tautology

`archetype.secondary` widens and is declared. Both anchors are required leaves carrying the enum members named, so neither leg fails open on a map that omits an optional field.

The type-level trigger is `data_ml.ml_involvement != 'none'`. This angle names a
PROPER SUBSET of what that admits, so a project can satisfy the type and leave this
angle false — which is the discrimination the gate exists to make. An angle whose
predicate merely restated the type trigger would fire on every survey and the
conditional marking would be decorative.

## What only this angle covers

The only angle that walks the corpus by ARTIFACT FORMAT rather than by task. That is the whole
reason it exists: a task-first walk returns the same server-class checkpoints the other angles
already carry, and an on-device candidate is defined by what it runs ON.

**Quantization is not a footnote, it is the identity of the artifact.** A 4-bit GGUF and its bf16
parent are different adoption decisions with different quality, and recording them as one candidate
loses the only fact this angle exists to find.

**Budget against 30 seconds, not 10.** The vendor catalogue's robots file sets a 10-second crawl
delay generally and 30 specifically for AI-agent user agents. The tighter number is the one that
applies to this survey.
